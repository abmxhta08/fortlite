LOADOUT

PISTOL
15 damage • semi-automatic
Modest range (50) and accuracy • unlimited ammo

You start with only the pistol, no grenades and no equipment. Two firearm slots (keys 1 and 2).

FOUND IN CHESTS

Each chest holds one random weapon — assault rifle, shotgun or SMG — and sometimes a medkit. Grenades (carry up to 3, G to throw) and shield potions lie on the ground. Defeated players drop their guns.

FOUND IN SUPPLY DROPS

Two drops land each match. Each holds one of: jetpack, sniper rifle or minigun.

SKINS

Click a skin to preview it on your character. Earn 1 coin for every victory.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Space: jump from the plane, then jump (hop) on the ground • WASD: steer your glider
  • WASD move • Mouse look • Left click shoot
  • R reload (every gun has a magazine; reserve ammo is unlimited; the minigun overheats instead) • 1 / 2 select a firearm slot • G throw a grenade • M mute • Esc pause
  • You start with a pistol. Look at a chest and press E to open it; press E at a weapon on the floor to pick it up (a full inventory swaps your active gun).
  • Shield potions (blue) add up to 100 shield; medkits come out of chests.
  • Four supply drops land each match (every ~30 s once the storm starts). Press E to open one — it holds a jetpack, sniper or minigun. Jetpack: hold Space while airborne. Sniper: right-click to zoom.
  • Stay inside the shrinking zone — the storm hurts everyone.
  • Last contestant standing wins 1 coin (Quick Match or Ranked).
  • Ranked: each elimination +5 • 1st +30 • 2nd–5th +15 • 6th–10th +5 • 11th–25th +0. A rank-based entry fee is paid when the match starts (Bronze 0, Silver 5, Gold 10, Platinum 15, Diamond 20, Champion 25). Ranks: Bronze 0, Silver 100, Gold 250, Platinum 500, Diamond 800, Champion 1,200 points. Quick Match never changes your points.
