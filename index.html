HOW TO PLAY

  • Space: jump from the plane, then jump (hop) on the ground • WASD: steer your glider (first or third person — V switches views while gliding)
  • WASD move • Mouse look • Left click shoot
  • R reload (every gun has a magazine; reserve ammo is unlimited; the minigun overheats instead; the shotgun loads one shell at a time and can fire as soon as one shell is in) • 1–5 select an inventory spot, I rearranges them (click with a medkit or potion held to use it) • Hold G to aim a grenade, release to throw • M mute • Esc pause
  • You start with a pistol. Look at a chest and press E to open it; press E at a weapon on the floor to pick it up (a full inventory swaps your active gun).
  • Shield potions (blue) add up to 100 shield; medkits come out of chests.
  • Four supply drops land each match (every ~30 s once the storm starts). Press E to open one — it holds a jetpack, sniper or minigun. Jetpack: hold Space while airborne. Sniper: right-click or Q toggles the scope.
  • New skins: Samurai Riley, Pablo, Trubin and Luke are the four featured Item Shop skins (10 coins each). Previously sold skins Crimson, Arctic, Jungle, Midnight and Golden were retired: owners keep them, nobody can buy them.
  • Item Shop: the SKINS page is now the ITEM SHOP (the Loadout page is gone): spend coins on character skins, and click any skin to preview it for free. Weapon wraps, gliders, contrails and back blings are Battle Pass rewards. Wins still award 1 coin.
  • Third-person gliding: glide in either camera (V switches while gliding); in third person your character hangs beneath the glider, hands on its handles.
  • Shotgun: now reloads one shell at a time (same total time from empty). Each shell is loaded immediately, and you can fire as soon as one is in.
  • Movement: Space jumps (about 1.7 m — enough to hop onto cars, crates and low ledges; a ledge up to ~2 m is climbed automatically) • C toggles crouch (slower, smaller target, you can't stand up under a low ceiling) • V switches first / third person (also in Settings).
  • Grapple gun (rare chest find): aim at a solid surface — the crosshair turns green when it can attach — and left-click. 45 m range, 5 charges, 1 s cooldown; a charge is only spent on a successful hook. Click again or switch item to let go.
  • Shockwave grenade (rare chest find, stack of 3): hold G to aim, release to throw. After 1 s it launches everyone nearby — including you — without any damage. Walls block it.
  • Shield damage flashes the screen edge blue, health damage red; a wedge shows which way the hit came from, and the storm pulses without a direction.
  • Maps rotate Meadow Town → Haunted Hollow → Dustfall Canyon, a desert town: Dusty Junction (central town, most chests), Scrap Valley (scrapyard), Redrock Outpost (military compound), Oasis Springs (a quiet pond) and Redrock Mine, an abandoned quarry under the road bridge. The quarry is 3 m deep with four stair-ramps out (west, north, east, south), benches, rock formations, mine carts and a small timber-framed mine entrance with loot. You can also drop off the rim, or grapple / shockwave across. The pond is shallow and walkable.
  • Corner landmarks: every map has two new quiet landing spots — Meadow Town: Camp Evergreen (NW corner) and Sunny Acres (NE corner); Haunted Hollow: Withered Orchard (NE) and Blackwater Mill (SW), with the graveyard (Crypt Hollow) now north of the manor; Dustfall Canyon: Dead-End Drive-In (NW) and Sunburn Solar (south edge). Towers and the barn loft have ordinary stairs; creeks and ponds are shallow and walkable.
  • Meadow Town now has Meadow Mall in the middle (two shopping wings round a courtyard: market, clothes shop, sports shop and café, with roof stairs and a big pylon sign) and Meadow Pitch, a soccer field with goals, bleachers, benches and an equipment shed, to the east. Haunted Hollow has Hollow Manor: a two-floor mansion with a grand staircase, balcony, outside stairs, a cellar under the garden and gated grounds. Dustfall Canyon has Dustfall Speedway, a dirt race track with pit garage, bleachers and a low jump, north of the mine. The safe zone no longer always ends in the middle of Meadow Town and Haunted Hollow.
  • Stay inside the shrinking zone — the storm hurts everyone.
  • Last contestant standing wins 1 coin (Quick Match or Ranked).
  • Ranked: each elimination +5 • 1st +30 • 2nd–5th +15 • 6th–10th +5 • 11th–25th +0. A rank-based entry fee is paid when the match starts (Bronze 0, Silver 5, Gold 10, Platinum 15, Diamond 20, Champion 25, Legendary 30). Ranks: Bronze 0, Silver 100, Gold 250, Platinum 500, Diamond 800, Champion 1,200, Legendary 5,000 points. Bots get sharper with every division (faster reactions, steadier aim, longer sight, smarter use of cover) but have the same health and damage as everyone, and even Legendary bots miss. Quick Match never changes your points.

WHAT'S NEW IN v1.2

FORTLITE VERSION 1.2
  • Four new skins (10 coins each): Samurai Riley (red armour), Pablo (sky-blue & white striped jersey), Trubin (black track suit and ushanka) and Luke (all-black tactical). Crimson, Arctic, Jungle, Midnight and Golden are retired from the shop — owners keep and can still equip them.
  • Item Shop & Battle Pass: SKINS is now the ITEM SHOP (the Loadout tab is gone): three featured skins and a skin catalog with free previews. The pass grows to 30 tiers: player banners were replaced by 12 new cosmetics — weapon wraps (Crimson Stripe, Arctic Blue, Golden Touch), gliders (Sunset Wing, Batwing, Desert Patrol), contrails (Sky Streak, Ember Drop, Phantom Trail) and back blings (Trail Pack, Patch Pumpkin Pack, Mini Bot) — plus a Haunted Legend title.
  • New character models: every player and bot is now a more detailed, rounded humanoid with a face, hair, hands, boots and clothing details; far-away bots use a lighter model.
  • New landmarks: Meadow Mall and Meadow Pitch (soccer field) in Meadow Town, Hollow Manor in Haunted Hollow, and Dustfall Speedway (dirt race track beside Redrock Mine). Each has chests, cover and several ways in and out, and the final safe zone now varies on the Meadow and Hollow maps.
  • Six new corner landmarks: Camp Evergreen (campground with a ranger tower) and Sunny Acres (farm with a barn loft) in Meadow Town, Withered Orchard (scarecrow landmark) and Blackwater Mill (sawmill by a creek) in Haunted Hollow, and Dead-End Drive-In (giant faded screen) and Sunburn Solar (solar field) in Dustfall Canyon. Hollow's graveyard moved well away from Hollow Manor.
  • Victory Royale & defeat screens: winning plays a #1 VICTORY ROYALE banner with confetti and a results panel; losing shows YOU PLACED #X with its own results.
  • Graphics refresh: brighter, more saturated colours and warmer sunlight.
  • Ranked entry fees: Champion 25 coins, Legendary 30 coins. Meadow Mall lost its big red roof and entrance signs. Chest loot was rebalanced and swapping held items with a full inventory was fixed.

PREVIOUS UPDATE
  • New map: Dustfall Canyon — five named locations (Dusty Junction, Scrap Valley, Redrock Outpost, Oasis Springs and Redrock Mine): a desert town, scrapyard, military outpost, oasis and an abandoned quarry with stair-ramps, a timber-framed mine entrance and a road bridge overhead.   • Minigun buff: +15% damage, 30% faster spin-up, 20% tighter spread.
  • Grapple gun and Shockwave grenade: new rare chest finds. Swing to ledges, or launch everyone nearby with zero damage.
  • Ranked: new Legendary division at 5,000 points and Champion entry fee now 30.
  • Damage feedback: blue/red screen edges, hit-direction wedges, trailing health bars, new hit sounds and a SHIELD BROKEN alert.
