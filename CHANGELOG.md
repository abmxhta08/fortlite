# FortLite changelog

## v2.0 "The Meteors Have Landed" (Season 2, fall): map changes

In v1.4 the meteors hung over the maps. In Season 2 they have landed: every big meteor left a crater with a small research POI inside, and the small ones left houses damaged but standing. Map changes only: the Item Shop, Battle Pass, skins, weapons, ranked, accounts and duel code are untouched (the duel arena build is split off, see below). The v1.4 news popup is not rewritten yet.

### Meteors gone, lobby shard
- All hanging meteors, halos, tails, ground-glow decals and the three meteor showers (Meadow Town, Harvest Hollow, Dustfall Canyon) are removed; the camera's 600 m far plane stays. (`aimRock` is kept, unused.)
- Lobby: a 5.2 m glowing meteor shard (purple / blue emissive core spikes, rock chunks, a pulsing halo) stands on a scorched patch at (3.4, 0, -9), far behind the hero (z = -9, so it is always behind every character and Locker preview). It and its patch are hidden while skin thumbnails are rendered.

### Craters
One shared helper (`craterBuild`) builds every Season 2 crater with the v1.4 Hollow mechanism: a lowered disc (`qmask`, floor -3 m), a 2 m rock wall ring, 4 m wide stair ramps (11 steps of 0.25 m), scorched rim rocks with glowing cracks, glowing shards and floor cracks, a minimap outline, and roads cut at the rim (circular cut). Ramps are entered from their ends only: the floor cells beside a ramp's upper part are kept off the bots' paths (the nav grid has no heights, so a bot would otherwise walk into the ramp's side face).
- **Meadow Town: Mallfall Crater** (replaces Meadow Mall): centre (0, 0), radius 24 m (ring to 26), four ramps (W, E, N, S along the old road axes). **Meteor Research Lab** inside: two storeys (stairs ground -> floor 2 -> roof deck), four doors, an atrium with a glowing meteor core in a containment ring (posts + emissive bands), a three-tier dome on the roof, a scaffold tower with a deck and stairs, two generator trailers, crates and work lights (emissive boxes, no dynamic lights). Rim remains: a half-collapsed store wall with a hanging awning, shopping carts, toppled planters, cracked parking-lot slabs. Chests: 8 spots (3 players only), 6 per match (was 8 / 6).
- **Meadow Town: Skyfall Pit** (replaces Skyline Square, name and minimap label): centre (-76.5, 76.5), radius 16 m, ramps E, N, S. **Containment Outpost**: a fenced dig site round a medium shard with floodlight masts, a small crane, two enterable cabins, a watch platform at 3.5 m with stairs. Rim remains: foundation stubs with rebar, concrete chunks and one tower corner (a walkable first floor at 3.3 m reached by an outside stair, two storeys of wall). Chests: 8 spots (2 players only), 7 per match (was 8 / 7).
- **Dustfall Canyon: Junction Crater** (replaces Redrock Mine, the quarry, its four ramps, benches, formations, carts, the mine entrance, the hanging bus and the road bridge with its second walking layer; the old minimap labels and `deck` nav layer are gone): centre (1.5, 1), radius 31 m (ring to 33), four ramps. Every building / prop whose footprint touches the crater is removed (about five buildings of Dusty Junction, the gas station); the roadside dinosaur now lies toppled and the water tower is bent and leaning (both decorative: nothing new to climb). The main roads and dirt paths stop at the rim. **Excavation Site**: a drilling rig (enterable base, roof deck, derrick with the drill pipe), a conveyor ramp up to a loading platform with a hopper, mine carts on short rails, two prefab cabins, a fenced shard pile. Chests: 8 spots (2 players only), 5 per match (the mine had 5 + a bus chest). Speedway untouched.
- **Harvest Hollow: Shattered Isle**: the manor is gone completely (house, porch, balcony, staircase, kitchen wing, cellar, roofs, chimneys, with their collision; the cellar hole is filled, the slab is solid). The island, its underside, veins, fragments, launch pads and physics are unchanged, and so is the crater below. Island top: a shallow impact scar (stacked dark decals, glowing cracks, rim rocks) with a 7.5 m glowing shard where the house stood, charred foundation stones (cover), a broken fence, a cracked fountain, gravestones, pumpkins and three snapped trunks. In the crater: a research camp (three tents, a table with a glowing sample jar, a generator, crates, work lights), nothing taller than 3.4 m. Chests keep the 11-spot total (5 on the island, players only; 6 in the camp, bot-reachable), 7 per match. Bots still never target or loot the island.

### Broken houses (small meteors)
On every map exactly two houses in three are hit (a seeded shuffle per map, so the same houses every match). Damage is real geometry from the same solids list: a 2 x 2 m hole in the roof (the slab is rebuilt from four pieces, charred rim, a broken beam), a missing back corner (stubs stay, the wall and its corner board above 1.1 m are gone), scorched wall patches, a small crater beside the house (decal, seven rim rocks, a little glowing shard), a rubble heap, a few broken fence pickets. The doorway side, door clearings, roads, roof stairs and chest spots are never touched (houses with a roof stair are skipped). Piece colours reuse the existing instanced colours; own RNG, so the map's seeded layout does not shift.

### Autumn

Round trees are recoloured per tree (position hash: about 28% stay green, the rest orange, red or gold), plus leaf piles and scattered leaves round the trees, hay bales behind some houses, pumpkins by some doors, wreaths, three scarecrows, corn stalk bundles and orange / red / gold bunting at Camp Evergreen and Sunny Acres.

### Duel arena

Verified: the arena's solids, roofs, nav cells, chest areas and place names are identical to v1.4 even after the Season 2 maps were built first.

### Layout stability
- Everything outside the new craters and the damaged houses is unchanged: Meadow Town's far-field solids, roofs, nav cells and chest candidates are bit-identical to v1.4, Harvest Hollow's too (the old manor fence still draws from the seeded RNG so later POIs do not shift), Dustfall's too apart from the dozen buildings the crater swallowed. Season 2 code never advances the map's seeded RNG.

### v2.0, second batch: collisions, island bots, new weapons, two new POIs, fall lobby, Season 2 pass

**Everything solid.** A collision audit runs when every map is built: any standing decoration at least 0.7 m tall and 0.25 m wide that rests on the ground and is not already covered by a solid becomes a solid with the same box (crater rim rocks, shards, lamp posts, barrels and so on: 56 / 66 / 18 extra solids on Meadow / Hollow / Dustfall). Leaf piles, decals, pumpkins, grass, corn, flags and the island's floating fragments stay walk-through; tree colliders are the trunk only. The same solids drive movement, nav, bullets and explosions; the nav grid is regenerated with them. Chests that would sit inside a new collider are dropped (none were), and the pad beacons (tall poles) are excluded so a pad's centre stays free. The lobby has no collision (it is a backdrop).

**Bots on Shattered Isle** (this overrides the v1.4 rules). The island top is a bot walking layer (3 m margin from the rim). Each launch pad is a one-way nav link to the island top: a bot walks onto the pad and releases all input, the pad launches it, and it lands about 9 m inside the rim. Eleven rim cells are one-way drops down to the ground / crater floor (each in a gap of the island's broken fence), so bots walk off the edge. Island landing targets were added and the glide-off logic removed; the island chests are bot-usable. Simulated 7-minute matches: bots land on the island and reach it by the pads (8 pad launches in one run, 7 different bots on the island, 4 of 4 island chests looted, none frozen at the pads or the rim; one stuck-at-the-fence case was found and fixed by moving the drop points into the fence gaps).

**Weapons** (existing guns unchanged). Burst Rifle: 22 damage per round (AR 20), 3-round burst 0.06 s apart, first round spread 0.008 (AR 0.012), rounds 2 and 3 spread 0.017, 0.5 s between bursts, 30-round magazine, 2.4 s reload, range 70. Hand Cannon: 75 damage, 0.8 s between shots, 7 rounds, 2.0 s reload, spread 0.004 standing or crouched and 0.035 moving or jumping, range 45, big kick, heavy sound. Tactical Shotgun: 6 pellets of 8.4 (the pump does 6 x 12: 70% per shot), 0.5 s between shots (1.8x the pump's rate), 8 shells, 0.4 s per shell, spread 0.095 (pump 0.07), range 16, the same crosshair tightening as the pump. Each has an original model built from the shared part system (first and third person, floor pickups, bot guns and weapon wraps all work), a hotbar / kill-feed icon, its own fire sound, and bot support (ratings: Burst 4.5, Cannon 4 like the AR, Tactical 3 like the pump; bots hold the Cannon at 14 m, never fire it beyond 45 m and use shotguns up close). Chest odds (percent): AR 17, Burst 15, Pump 16, Tactical 15, SMG 19, Hand Cannon 13, Pumpkin RPG 5. Floor weapons are drawn equally from the six non-RPG guns. The duel kit is unchanged.

**Silo Nine and Cider Stacks (Harvest Hollow).** The planned corners are taken (NW by Hollow Mill at (-67, -67), SE by Moonlit Manor at (73, 72), NE by Withered Orchard, SW by Blackwater Mill), so no existing POI was touched and the two new ones stand on the empty edge areas: **Silo Nine** on the west edge (zone x -97..-66, z 0..32, centre about (-82, 16)) and **Cider Stacks** on the east edge (x 62..97, z -17..19, centre about (79, 1)), with 3+ m gaps from Dead Oak Road, Eclipse Observatory, Cursed Quarry and Pumpkin Patch. A few generic houses and trees inside the zones were cleared (as for the v1.3 corner POIs). Silo Nine: fenced compound (openings on all four sides, a guard gate with a barrier arm and booth), a missile 18 m tall standing in a ring with open blast doors, hazard stripes, a grated glowing floor and green barrels, a two-storey command building with a radar dish (roof player-only), two bunkers, a watchtower, trucks, sandbags, and a locked-looking **command vault** whose chest always gives a Hand Cannon. Cider Stacks: two 16 m cooling stacks with enterable ground floors (a chest each) and pooled steam sprites, a factory hall with catwalks and a rare chest on the top catwalk (always a Burst Rifle, Hand Cannon or Tactical Shotgun), tanks, a press, conveyors, apple crates, a loading dock with trucks, and **two steam vents** (the pad code with a straight-up launch, a little drift toward the hall roof) with one-way nav links to the roof and drops back down. Each POI has 7 chest spots and 6 are used per match, so Harvest Hollow's chest total goes from 33 to 45 (the chest pool grows from 40 to 48). Both get 2-3 landings by default; the stacks and the missile are visible from the drop. Steam is halved on Low graphics. The map card art, minimap labels and supply-drop rules follow the other POIs.

**Fall lobby.** A golden-orange sky with a low sun, orange / red / gold trees, leaf piles, pumpkins, hay bales and drifting leaves behind the hero (all at z <= -3.2, hidden while skin thumbnails render); warm lights; amber / brown UI accents (layout unchanged). The meteor shard stays.

**Season 2 Battle Pass "FALLEN".** A new 20-tier pass (the pass screen, claim logic and cloud merge use 20 tiers; Season 1 cosmetics and skins stay owned and equippable from the Locker). Saves carry `s: 2`; a Season 1 save is migrated once (its earned-but-unclaimed tiers are claimed with a toast, Season 2 XP starts at 0); cloud merges migrate both sides first, so an old cloud save can never pour Season 1 XP into Season 2. The tier list is exactly as specified; Starfall (charred armour with unlit purple-blue cracks, a meteor shard in the chest, an ice arm, a torn scarf, glowing eyes; built on the shared rig with first-person arms and hitboxes unchanged) is the only skin and unlocks at tier 20 together with the "Fallen Legend" title. **XP per tier is 150, not 300:** a match gives 25 (completion) + 10 per elimination + 25 (top five) + 75 (victory), about 55 on average for an average player, so 300 x 20 = 6000 XP would take about 105 matches; 150 x 20 = 3000 XP takes about 55, the target. XP per match is unchanged. Ranked points, divisions and the ranked leaderboard are untouched.

### v2.0, fixes: Hand Cannon, blocked entrances, endgame bots, glider landing, signs

**Hand Cannon accuracy.** Spread standing or crouched is 0.0004 (was 0.004), walking 0.0175 (half the old 0.035), jumping or falling 0.035 (unchanged); range 45 -> 100 m. Spread is not cumulative, so accuracy is already back to full before the next shot (0.8 s). The game has no damage falloff for pistols (only the shotguns have it), so it already keeps full damage at any range; damage, fire rate, magazine, reload and recoil are unchanged, no other gun and no bot aim error changed. Tested (400 shots each, aimed at the body centre of a bot): 20 m standing / crouched / walking / jumping 100 / 100 / 100 / 59%, 50 m 100 / 100 / 45 / 23%, 80 m 100 / 100 / 27 / 4%.

**Blocked entrances.** Cause at Cider Stacks: the two catwalk stairs ran along z = -4.7 / 4.7 straight into the hall's north and south doors at x = 82. The doors moved to x = 88 (window to x = 80) and the loading dock has a gap in front of the south door. New automated check (a test harness, not shipped): a capsule walk through every doorway, fence opening, stair, crater ramp and house door in both directions (centre and side lanes), a nav-connectivity check for both sides of each opening, and glider drops through house doors; it uses the opening records the map builder now keeps (`rec`). It found and fixed: Silo Nine's guard-booth door and south gate blocked by sandbags and a generated crate, the north and west fence openings that led straight into the command building and a bunker (now closed), Skyfall Pit's west opening that led into the watch platform (now closed), plus a general rule: on every map, any prop that is not a wall or a stair (crate, sandbag, rubble, hay, barrel, generated clutter) standing within 1.5 m in front of or behind a doorway or fence opening is removed with its collision. Result: 0 failures in every new or changed POI on all three maps (Mallfall Crater and its lab, Skyfall Pit, Junction Crater and the Excavation Site, Shattered Isle and the crater camp, Silo Nine, Cider Stacks, all crater ramps, all broken houses). The sweep still reports older POIs (v1.2 / v1.3: Maplewood Academy, Camp Evergreen, Sunny Acres, Dreadrail Depot, Cursed Quarry and some Dustfall perimeter openings: 12 / 28 / 5 items on Meadow / Hollow / Dustfall, some of them stair end-point artefacts of the test); those were not touched.

**Endgame bots.** Reproduced with 20 trials per map (land, keep 3 bots, close the storm, log each bot every 0.5 s; a bot counts as broken if it moves under 1.2 m in 6 s while not fighting and 4+ m from the final point). Causes: (1) bots that landed on a roof, ledge or the island had a ground path but could not step off (no walking layer, parapets), (2) bots standing in a blocked cell beside a crater ramp or the cliff had no path at all (path = none), so nothing ever moved them, (3) the old stuck handler only worked below 0.3 m height and only after a path existed; target flip-flopping was also possible. Fixes: the final safe-zone centre is snapped to open, reachable, ground-level main-area ground (not a crater, the island, a roof or within 1.5 m of a collider); stuck detection every 0.5 s: after about 1.5 s repath, then sidestep and jump, then walk straight at the goal for 1.5 s, and after about 4 s step to the nearest free connected ground cell (6 intervals for bots up on roofs or the island) and pick a new nearby goal; a visible target is kept for at least 2.5 s. No combat logic was changed and no division setting was touched. Broken-bot trials out of 20: Meadow 2 -> 1, Hollow 6 -> 1, Dustfall 2 -> 1.

**Glider landing on the roof.** Cause: the glide landing used the highest solid under the glider at that height plus 0.5 m (and also the roof's top at that x / z), so a glider flying through a doorway or under an eave below the roof slab was lifted onto the roof. The landing surface is now the solid directly below the glider's previous height (floors count, roof slabs count only when the glider is above them). Normal falling, jumping, launch pads, steam vents and drops use the same downward check and the collision push is sideways, so they were not affected; the harness glides bots through every house door at 2.7 m (all land on the floor) on all three maps.

**Signs with words.** Every sign, banner and board with letters is gone from all maps including the duel arena (Meadow Mall store signs, the lab, outpost and silo signs, the Academy, Library, Corner Store, Ranger Station, Tickets, Snacks, Solar, Star Chart, Eclipse, Dreadrail, Keep Out, Cider, Cider Stacks, Excavation Site, Now Showing, Dustfall Speedway, Lap board, scoreboard); their collision went with them. The posts that only held signs (scoreboard, race gantry, lap board) were removed. Shape-only decoration (hazard stripes, awnings, emblems, colour boards) stays, HUD / minimap / menus untouched.

**Tactical Shotgun out of the loot pool (for now).** It stays fully in the game files (stats, model, icon, sounds, bot support) but no chest, floor spot or rare chest gives it. Chest odds (percent): AR 20, Burst 18, Pump 19, SMG 22, Hand Cannon 16, Pumpkin RPG 5. To bring it back, see the comment above `CHEST_ODDS` in `index.html`.

**Silo Nine moved again:** it now stands where Hollow Mill was (north-west, centre about (-67, -67), zone x -83..-52, z -83..-51). Hollow Mill is removed (its place name and every generated building and prop of its site); the top edge is plain terrain again.

**Dropped guns last 10 s.** A gun dropped by an eliminated contestant now stays 10 seconds before it despawns (was 5). Medkits, shields, grenades and guns you swap out yourself still never expire.

- **Login news popup:** the news popup now shows Season 2 with snapshots of the Hand Cannon, the Trojan skin and Mallfall Crater (Meadow Town), plus "Duos coming soon..." at the bottom. It opens when the game loads and again after a fresh log in.

- **Starfall skin redesign:** now a horned white helmet with a glowing cyan visor slit, purple collar and chest with cyan trim, teal arms and cuffs, a long purple-to-teal coat and white shoes (replaces the charred-armour look).

- **Dev note, Director mode (not shown in the lobby):** open the game with `?director` (for example `index.html?director`) to get the trailer shot recorder. It is not linked from any menu. While it runs, nothing is written to storage (saves, coins, XP, stats, ranked and the leaderboard are untouched) and the accounts / online services are off. Shots are defined in one list (`SHOTS` inside `directorMode()`): `meteor_intro`, `orbit_mallfall`, `orbit_skyfall`, `orbit_junction`, `orbit_isle`, `burst_1`, `burst_2`, `cannon_1`, `cannon_2`, `trojan_turn`. Actors are bots with `remote = true` (no AI) moved by a script; the player is script-driven and every shot and hit goes through the real weapon code; `Math.random` is seeded per shot. PREVIEW plays a shot, RECORD ONE / RECORD ALL render it at 1920 x 1080, High graphics, a fixed 1/60 s step and encode every frame with WebCodecs H.264 into MP4 (mp4-muxer, loaded from a CDN only in director mode); each clip downloads when its shot ends. Without WebCodecs it falls back to canvas capture + MediaRecorder (WebM, real time) and says so on screen. Keep the tab visible while recording.

### 1v1 Duel (online)
- New **1V1 DUEL** button under PLAY in the lobby. **CREATE ROOM** makes a 4-letter room code (letters and digits without look-alikes); the other player types it under **JOIN**. A room holds exactly two players: a third gets "That room is already full", a wrong code gets "No room with that code". No account is needed (guests are named GuestNNN, logged-in players use their username).
- Both players press **READY**, then a 3-second countdown (movement and shooting locked) starts a match that is **first to 3 round wins**. A round ends when one player goes down; two eliminations within 0.7 s count as a draw and the round is replayed. After the match: **Rematch** or back to the lobby. If the opponent leaves, the match ends with "OPPONENT LEFT".
- **Arena:** the Meadow Town map with its mall, trimmed to a walled 88 x 88 m square (12 m walls, collision up to the sky) so there is open ground, houses, cars and trees around the mall. The minimap greys out everything outside the walls. The players spawn at opposite ends (north / south, facing each other). No chests, no ground loot, no supply drops and no storm.
- **Kit:** assault rifle (30 rounds), shotgun (5 shells), one stack of 2 shield potions, 100 health and 100 shield, reset every round.
- **How it works:** Supabase Realtime (broadcast + presence) on a channel named after the room code; the game opens its own anonymous connection straight to Supabase, because the Netlify proxy cannot carry websockets. About 12 small messages a second per player (position, health, shots). The opponent is one of the bot slots driven by those messages (no AI). Each game decides its own player's health: the shooter reports hits, the target applies them and reports its own death.
- **School-friendly rooms:** if the live (websocket) connection is blocked, CREATE ROOM switches by itself to a slower connection made of ordinary requests (through the same `/sb` proxy that logins use), and there is also a manual **SCHOOL-FRIENDLY ROOM** button. Those room codes start with a digit; a code starting with a letter is a live room. It needs a `duel_msgs` table in Supabase (SQL below in the setup notes); about 7 updates a second instead of ~12, so it feels laggier.
- **Duels leaderboard:** the LEADERBOARD tab now has two sub-tabs, **RANKED** (rank division and points, as before) and **DUELS** (every player by duel wins, plus your own position). A duel win is a match won by score (3 round wins); an opponent leaving does not count. Duel wins are stored in the career stats and the account's new `duel_wins` column (needs `alter table profiles add column if not exists duel_wins int not null default 0;`). Only logged-in players appear.
- Duels do not touch coins, battle pass XP or ranked points (duel wins are only counted for the duels leaderboard). Pausing (Esc) does not pause the duel and a paused player can still be hit.
- Known limits: a modified client could cheat (it is peer to peer; a server would be needed to prevent that), the opponent is shown as a coloured bot rather than their skin, and networks that block websockets to supabase.co cannot play.

### Accounts, cloud saves and leaderboard
- Optional email + password accounts (Supabase Auth) with a username shown on the leaderboard. A login popup appears after the news popup with **Play as guest**; Settings has an ACCOUNT section with LOG OUT, and the nav shows LOG IN / your name.
- Coins, owned skins, equipped skin, battle pass (XP, claimed tiers, equipped items), career stats and ranked points are saved to the account a couple of seconds after every change (and when the tab closes), and loaded on login. If the connection drops, changes stay on the device and sync later. The first login adds the guest progress on this device to the account once; logging out returns the device to a clean guest.
- New **LEADERBOARD** tab: every player by ranked points, showing each player's rank division and points, plus your own position. It needs a `rank_points` column on the Supabase `profiles` table; scores are reported by the browser, so a determined player can edit them (no server-side verification yet).
- Network friendliness: the sign-in library ships with the game (`supabase.min.js`, CDN fallback) and on a `*.netlify.app` address all account traffic goes through the same site at `/sb/*` (the `_redirects` file forwards it to Supabase), so school filters that block third-party hosts still allow login. The LOG IN button is always shown, with a clear message when the service cannot be reached.
- Passwords are handled by Supabase (hashed, never stored by the game); the page only contains the public anon key and row-level security limits each player to their own row. The game still works offline and with the sign-in library blocked.

### Pumpkin RPG
- New chest weapon on all three maps: **15% of chest openings** award it (rolled once when the chest opens, independently per chest); the other 85% is split equally between the assault rifle, shotgun and SMG exactly as before (28.3% each). Still one weapon plus one collectible per chest.
- Orange-and-black compact launcher with a jack-o'-lantern warhead that slides in on reload and disappears after firing; the rocket is a glowing jack-o'-lantern with a short spark trail. Works in first and third person with every skin and wrap, has a hotbar icon, `1/2` ammo display, its own launch sound, and kill-feed icon.
- Numbers: 1 rocket per magazine + 3 spare (4 in total), 3.2 s reload, rocket speed 24 m/s (dodgeable), 3.2 s lifetime, 5.5 m blast radius, a flat 120 damage to everyone inside it (centre or edge), 1 s between shots. Explodes once on ground, any solid or a contestant; solid cover blocks splash; you take damage from your own blast. Brief blast flash, particles and a small camera shake; buildings are not damaged.
- Bots can use it through the normal weapon system (rating equal to the SMG / shotgun): they never fire it closer than 5 m, switch away when it is empty, and aim with the same error as for bullets. Dropped RPGs keep their loaded rocket. Everything is cleared on match end.

## v1.1

### New map: Dustfall Canyon
- A third battle-royale map: a desert town built around Redrock Mine, a shallow (3 m) quarry with an abandoned quarry with a real lower floor, stair-ramps, a road bridge and cliff walls. Built in the existing low-poly building, tree, prop and weapon style.
- Five named locations: **Dusty Junction** (central town: motel, gas station with a roadside dinosaur, mechanic's garage, houses with roof stairs, water tower), **Scrap Valley** (rusty car rows, enterable shipping containers, tyre stacks, crusher), **Redrock Outpost** (fenced compound, warehouse, barracks, a modest watchtower), **Oasis Springs** (shallow walkable pond, palms, colourful courtyards) and **Redrock Mine** (an irregular 3 m deep quarry under the road bridge: four stair-ramps, two rock benches, rock formations, carts on short rails, and a timber-framed mine entrance alcove with loot; an abandoned bus hangs over its edge).
- Other landmarks: crashed supply plane near the east edge, signs, distant decorative mesas outside the playable boundary.
- Maps now rotate Meadow Town → Haunted Hollow → Dustfall Canyon (existing saves keep working).
- Bots navigate ramps, the bridge deck (a second walking layer) and the canyon floor; supply drops, chests and bot destinations are only placed on reachable ground.

### Ranked bots rebalanced (about 30% less combat effectiveness in every division)
- Aim error retuned per division (measured, not randomly discarded hits). Champion and Legendary no longer have perfect aim; their reaction times and aim-tracking speed were also eased. The difficulty order Bronze < Silver < Gold < Platinum < Diamond < Champion < Legendary is preserved.
- No change to weapon damage, bot health, ranked points, thresholds, entry costs or rewards.

### v1.1 changes
- Movement: jump height 1.7 m, standing on cars / crates / roofs, mantling ledges up to ~2 m, crouch (C).
- Third-person camera (V / Settings) with obstruction pull-in.
- Sniper scope toggle (right-click or Q).
- Minigun buff (+15% damage, -30% spin-up, -20% spread).
- Grapple gun and Shockwave grenade (rare chest finds).
- Ranked: Legendary division at 5,000 points (entry fee 30), Champion entry fee 25, per-division bot profiles.
- Incoming-damage feedback: blue / red edge pulses, hit-direction wedges, trailing bars, shield-break cue.

### Third-person gliding and shotgun reload (added to v1.1)
- Gliding works in both camera views and respects the saved preference; V switches views while gliding. In third person the character hangs below the equipped glider (a striped canopy, or the Bat Wings) with both hands on its grips, and the camera sits behind and above so the landing area stays visible.
- The shotgun reloads one shell at a time: the empty-to-full time is unchanged (2.4 s, split over the 5 shells), each shell is added to the magazine the moment it is inserted, and the weapon can fire as soon as one shell is loaded (firing cancels the rest of the reload). Switching, dropping, dying or leaving a match cancels cleanly and keeps the shells already loaded. Bots reload the same way.
